WHY CAMPUSCONNECT?

Make volunteering easier to discover.

CampusConnect is a student project concept designed to bring community initiatives into one simple, welcoming space. It encourages students to explore local causes, collaborate with peers and take part in meaningful activities.

The current version is a frontend demonstration. Event data and registrations are stored locally in your browser and are not shared with other users.
